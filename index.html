FAQ

Questions

Is the checklist really free?

Yes. Sign up to get it by email along with Maker Ops Weekly, or preview the PDF directly.

Is this official advice from Bambu Lab, Etsy or Shopify?

No. The Maker Runbook is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by any platform. We link to the official pages so you can check them, because platform rules change often.

Do I need a Bambu Lab printer?

No. The Bambu runbook is the most detailed one, but the triage steps, customer templates, backlog tracker and audit work with any printer.

Can I sell prints of licensed STL files on Etsy?

Etsy's Creativity Standards say that items made with a 3D printer must be based on the seller's own original design. A commercial licence for someone else's file doesn't change that. The Etsy Sell Kit explains this in detail. Check Etsy's current policy before listing.

Is this legal advice?

No. We summarise UK consumer rules, such as delivery times and cancellation rights, and link to the official sources. For your own situation, check GOV.UK or get professional advice.
